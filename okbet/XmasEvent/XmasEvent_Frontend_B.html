            Light Up Your Christmas Luck!

            How to Play

            1. Santa's Daily Gift

            Check In Daily, Unwrap More Surprises!

            Check in every day to collect 50 Points! Keep your streak going to unlock a Mystery Chest every day from Day 3 onwards.

            Mystery Chests may contain Points, Cash, Free Spins, or Golden Draw Tickets!

            2. Parol Quest

            Complete Missions, Collect Parols!

            Place bets or make deposits to earn Sparks and complete festive missions.

            Unlock Cash Bonuses and collect Parols to light up your Christmas rewards!

            3. Parol Wish Draw

            Collect Parols, Draw Your Christmas Wishes!

            Use your Parols to enter any of four festive prize pools for a chance to win exciting rewards, including Cash Bonuses, Free Spins, Vouchers, and physical prizes!

            The more Parols you collect, the more chances you have to win!

            Terms & Conditions

            1. Santa's Daily Gift

            1.1 Daily Check-In

            Each user may check in once per day during the promotion period. Check-in eligibility resets daily at 00:00 (UTC+8, Philippine Time).

            Check-in rewards are based on consecutive check-in days:

            Consecutive DaysDaily Rewards
            Day 150 Points
            Day 250 Points
            Day 3 onwards50 Points + 1 Mystery Chest

            From Day 3 onwards, users may continue receiving 50 Points and 1 Mystery Chest each day, provided they maintain their consecutive check-in streak.

            Missing a daily check-in will reset the streak. The next successful check-in will start again from Day 1.

            1.2 Mystery Chest Rewards

            Each Mystery Chest may contain one or more of the following rewards:

            • Points
            • Instant Cash
            • Free Spins
            • Golden Draw Tickets

            Rewards are randomly determined. Actual rewards will be displayed on the promotion page.

            2. Parol Quest

            Parol Quest features two mission categories: Christmas Mission and Daily Mission.

            Users can earn Sparks through eligible bets or successful deposits to complete missions and unlock rewards.

            2.1 Sparks Conversion Rates

            Sparks are calculated according to the following rates:

            CategorySparks Earned
            Slots Bets₱1 eligible bet = 1 Spark
            Live Casino Bets₱1 eligible bet = 0.5 Sparks
            Perya Bets₱1 eligible bet = 1 Spark
            Sports Bets₱1 eligible bet = 2 Sparks
            Deposit₱1 successful deposit = 1 Spark

            Only qualifying bets and successful deposits will count towards Sparks accumulation. Mission progress is subject to system verification.

            2.2 Christmas Mission

            Accumulate Sparks through eligible bets during the promotion period.

            For every 1,000 Sparks earned, users can receive the corresponding number of Parols based on the applicable mission requirements.

            Collected Parols can be used to participate in Parol Wish Draw.

            Specific mission milestones, reward quantities, and claiming requirements are displayed on the promotion page.

            2.3 Daily Mission

            Complete designated daily betting or deposit missions to unlock rewards.

            Depending on the mission level, rewards may include:

            • Cash Bonus: Credited directly to the user's wallet. A 1× wagering requirement on the bonus amount must be fulfilled before withdrawal.
            • Parols: Used to participate in Parol Wish Draw.

            Daily Mission progress resets at 00:00 (UTC+8, Philippine Time).

            Mission requirements and available rewards may vary by level. Please refer to the promotion page for details.

            2.4 PILI CARD Challenge

            Certain mission levels include a PILI CARD Challenge.

            When triggered, two cards will be presented. Users must select one card to reveal the result.

            If the selected card is higher than the other card, the user's Instant Cash reward will increase according to the applicable reward multiplier.

            If the selected card is lower than or equal to the other card, the original Instant Cash reward remains unchanged.

            Card rankings, from highest to lowest, are:

            A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2

            2.5 Reward & VIP Rules

            Not all mission levels offer every reward type. Available rewards depend on the corresponding mission requirements.

            If a user's VIP level increases during the promotion:

            • Claimed rewards: Remain unchanged.
            • Unclaimed rewards: Will be updated according to the user's current VIP level.

            Actual reward amounts and eligibility requirements are displayed on the promotion page.

            3. Parol Wish Draw

            3.1 How to Participate

            Collect Parols by completing eligible Parol Quest missions.

            Choose from four prize pools, each with its own Parol entry cost and available rewards.

            The number of Parols required per draw will be displayed on the promotion page.

            Parols used for a draw are non-refundable.

            All unused Parols will automatically expire at the end of the promotion and cannot be carried forward or exchanged for cash.

            3.2 Prize Distribution

            Digital Rewards

            Digital rewards, including Free Spins, Cash Bonuses, and Vouchers, will be credited directly to the user's account.

            Specific usage requirements and validity periods are subject to the applicable reward terms.

            Physical Prizes

            Winners of physical prizes will be contacted by OKGAMES Customer Support to confirm their delivery details.

            Physical prizes will be dispatched within one month after the required delivery information has been successfully confirmed.

            Delivery arrangements and shipping details may be confirmed with Customer Support.

            3.3 Physical Prize Claiming Deadline

            Winners must respond to Customer Support and provide the required delivery information within 28 days after the promotion ends.

            Failure to confirm the necessary information within this period will be considered a voluntary forfeiture of the physical prize.

            4. General Terms

            OKGAMES reserves the right to interpret these promotion terms, subject to applicable laws and regulatory requirements.
